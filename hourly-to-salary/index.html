The math behind it

annual = hourly × hours_per_week × weeks_per_year
weekly = hourly × hours_per_week
biweekly = weekly × 2 (26 paychecks a year)
monthly = annual ÷ 12 (not weekly × 4 — a month averages 4.333 weeks)

Example: $25/hr × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $52,000 a year — $1,000 a week, $2,000 per biweekly paycheck, and $4,333.33 a month. All figures are gross, before taxes and deductions. If some of your hours are paid at an overtime premium, this calculator understates your pay: it prices every hour at the same rate.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert an hourly wage to an annual salary?

Multiply your hourly wage by your hours per week, then by your weeks per year. The standard full-time case is $25/hr × 40 hours × 52 weeks = $52,000 a year. Adjust the weeks if you take unpaid time off or work seasonally.

Why is my monthly pay not just my weekly pay times 4?

Because a month is not 4 weeks — it averages 4.333 weeks (52 weeks ÷ 12 months). Monthly pay is annual pay ÷ 12. At $25/hr and 40 hours a week, weekly pay is $1,000 but monthly pay is $4,333.33, not $4,000.

How does biweekly pay differ from twice a month?

Biweekly means every two weeks, which gives 26 paychecks a year. Twice a month (semimonthly) gives 24 paychecks. At $1,000 a week, a biweekly check is $2,000 and there are two months a year with three paychecks.

Are these figures gross or net of taxes?

Gross. All figures are before income tax, Social Security, Medicare, and any benefits deductions. Your take-home pay will be lower, and the exact difference depends on your tax situation.

What if my hours vary week to week?

Enter your average hours per week. If your schedule swings a lot, run the calculator twice — once with your low week and once with your high week — to see the range your annual pay can land in.

Does this include overtime pay?

No. This calculator assumes every hour is paid at the same rate. If you regularly work overtime at a premium rate, use the SalaryLab overtime pay calculator to add that on top.
